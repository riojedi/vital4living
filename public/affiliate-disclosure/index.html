Affiliate disclosure

How Vital4Living earns from referrals — and the rules that keep recommendations honest.

Vital4Living may participate in affiliate programs, including Amazon Associates and outdoor-retailer programs (such as AvantLink, REI, and Backcountry). When we do, and you buy through a link on this site, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This disclosure applies on any page that contains affiliate links.

What that does not change

  • Recommendations are based on specs, fit, and buyer experience — not commission rates.
  • We do not invent product specifications. Specs come from manufacturers and retailers.
  • Higher commissions never move a product up a comparison.
  • Tools and calculators are the same whether or not an affiliate link is available.

Amazon Associates

If and when Vital4Living is enrolled as an Amazon Associate, it earns from qualifying purchases made through qualifying Amazon links on this site. Until enrollment is active, any Amazon links are non-affiliate and this notice will be updated.
