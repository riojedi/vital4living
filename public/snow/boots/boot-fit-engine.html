Boot Fit Engine

By Sierra Marlowe · Fit, Sizing & Standards

Ski boots are the most important — and most personal — purchase in the sport. Get a real starting point for mondo size, last width, volume class, flex index, and brands that tend to fit your foot profile. Built by a former ski-shop equipment buyer.

How this works

Mondo size is the shell length in centimeters — the global sizing standard. Your foot measures roughly 1 cm shorter than the mondo size, leaving room for the liner and a proper shell fit. Last width is the forefoot width the boot is built around. Volume class combines last width and instep to describe how snug or roomy the fit will be. Flex index is brand-relative, so treat it as a band, not an absolute. Brand recommendations reflect general fit tendencies across model lines — always confirm with the actual boot on your foot.
