Privacy policy

Last updated August 16, 2026. We run a content site and keep data collection to the minimum needed to operate it.

Vital4Living (“we,” “us,” “our”) publishes outdoor-equipment guides and tools at vital4living.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to being plain and honest about it.

In short

  • We do not require you to create an account to read our content.
  • We do not currently run advertising or third-party tracking (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no ad networks).
  • We may use affiliate links in the future; when we do, the retailer may set a cookie. See our affiliate disclosure.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing info@vital4living.com.

Information we collect

Directly from you. If you email us, we receive whatever you choose to send us — typically your name, email address, and the contents of your message. We use it only to reply to you.

Automatically. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) records standard server logs — such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and the pages you visit — so the site functions and stays secure. We do not currently tie these logs to a personal identity or an analytics account.

Cookies and tracking

As of this policy’s date, the site does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we add web analytics or join affiliate programs, we will update this section to name the service and the cookie, explain what it does, and tell you how to opt out. Essential preferences the site itself stores (such as your light/dark theme choice) are kept only in your browser and are not shared with us.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and provides security and caching.
  • Bing and Google receive our sitemap for search indexing.
  • Affiliate networks (such as Amazon Associates, AvantLink, REI, and Backcountry) may be used in the future. Clicking an affiliate link may set a cookie with that retailer so a commission can be credited. We do not control those networks’ cookies; their privacy policies apply.

Social media

We maintain a Facebook page. The site does not currently embed Facebook social plugins or use the Facebook pixel. If you message us through Facebook, Meta’s data practices apply to that interaction. You can disconnect our app from your Facebook account at any time at facebook.com/settings?tab=applications.

Our Facebook app

We use a Facebook app to publish to our own Facebook Page. If a page administrator authorizes the app, Meta may provide identifiers such as the Facebook Page ID, the administrator’s user ID, page permissions, and an access token. We use these only to manage and post to our own Facebook Page; we do not sell or share them, and we do not use them to track you across the web. To revoke access, remove the app at facebook.com/settings?tab=applications; to request deletion of any data we received through the app, email info@vital4living.com (see our data deletion instructions).

Your rights

If you are in the EU, UK, or Switzerland (GDPR), or a California resident (CCPA), you have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to opt out of future marketing. To exercise any of these rights, email info@vital4living.com with “Data request” in the subject line. We respond within 30 days (45 days where required by CCPA). See our data deletion instructions for details.

Data retention

We keep messages you send us only as long as needed to respond and handle any follow-up. Server log data is retained by our hosting provider according to its standard practices. When data is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date above reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be reflected here.

Contact

Email info@vital4living.com with any privacy questions or requests.
