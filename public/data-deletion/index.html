Data deletion instructions

Last updated August 16, 2026. You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you at any time.

This page explains how to request deletion of your personal data from Vital4Living. It also serves as the data deletion instructions URL for our Facebook app, as required by Meta’s platform policy.

What data we hold

We keep very little. In practice, we may have:

  • Any email or message you sent us (for example, to info@vital4living.com), along with your email address and the contents of your message.
  • Standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited) kept by our hosting provider.
  • If you connected our Facebook app: the data our app received when you linked your Facebook account (see below).

We do not currently maintain user accounts, a customer database, or a marketing email list.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@vital4living.com.
  2. Use the subject line “Data deletion request.”
  3. Tell us what data to delete (for example, “delete all data you hold about me”), and include the email address or account you want removed.

We confirm receipt within 30 days (45 days where required by California/CCPA) and delete the applicable data once we have verified your identity. You can also ask us what data we hold, or ask us to correct it.

Facebook-connected data

Our Facebook app is used only to manage and post to our own Facebook Page; we do not offer a Facebook Login on the site today. If a page administrator has authorized the app, Meta may have provided page and admin identifiers, page permissions, and an access token. We use these only to operate our own Page and do not sell or share them. To have app-related data deleted:

  • Go to facebook.com/settings?tab=applications and remove the Vital4Living app. This revokes our access to your Facebook information going forward.
  • Email us (above) to request deletion of any data we already received through the app. We delete it within 30 days.

Note: removing the app stops future access but does not automatically delete data we already hold — which is why a deletion request by email is the way to have it removed. If we ever add a Facebook Login to the site, we will update this page accordingly.

What we cannot delete

We cannot delete data we are required to keep for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention reasons, or information that has been de-identified so it no longer relates to you. Server logs held by our hosting provider are subject to that provider’s retention practices.

Questions

Email info@vital4living.com with any questions about deletion or your data. See our privacy policy for the full picture.
